# ⭐ Featured Analytics Projects

## 01 — RAIL-SHIELD: Passenger Journey Reliability Analytics

**MySQL · Python · Pandas · EDA · Power BI**

A journey-level reliability analytics concept that treats a passenger trip as a connected sequence of stations, segments, disruptions and transfer windows.

**Core problem:** Traditional delay reporting can miss how multiple events combine to create passenger journey friction.

**Analytics focus:**
- Journey delay
- Station and segment reliability
- Disruption contribution
- Connection pressure
- Journey Friction Score
- Operational investigation signals

**Project:** [projects/rail-shield](projects/rail-shield/)

---

## 02 — Citizen Friction Intelligence Engine

**Python · EDA · Excel · Power BI**

An operational analytics concept designed to measure hidden friction in service journeys through waiting time, repeat visits, abandonment and failure points.

**Core problem:** Complaints may reveal visible failures, while the time citizens lose inside a process can remain difficult to measure.

**Analytics focus:**
- Waiting-time patterns
- Repeat visits
- Abandonment
- Failure points
- Process-level friction indicators

**Project:** [projects/citizen-friction](projects/citizen-friction/)

---

## 03 — Ration Flow & Leakage Intelligence

**MySQL · EDA · Excel**

An analytical model connecting beneficiary entitlement, inventory movement and issued quantities to identify reconciliation gaps and unusual outlet-level variance.

**Core problem:** Distribution accountability requires connecting entitlement, stock and actual issue records instead of examining each record independently.

**Analytics focus:**
- Entitlement vs distribution
- Stock movement
- Reconciliation
- Outlet variance
- Anomaly signals

**Project:** [projects/ration-flow](projects/ration-flow/)

---

## 04 — Temple Operations Capacity Intelligence

**MySQL · Power BI · Statistics**

A multi-domain operations analytics model covering pilgrimage demand, darshan, seva, accommodation, booking, transport and queue events.

**Core problem:** High-volume visitor operations require capacity analysis across interconnected services rather than isolated counts.

**Analytics focus:**
- Demand by time slot
- Queue waiting
- Room utilization
- Service capacity
- Operational bottlenecks

**Project:** [projects/temple-operations](projects/temple-operations/)

---

## 05 — Inventory Silent-Loss Detector

**Python · EDA · MySQL**

An analytical workflow that compares receipts, transfers, picks, returns and closing stock to identify unusual movement and variance patterns.

**Core problem:** Some inventory loss signals may only become visible when multiple movement stages are reconciled.

**Analytics focus:**
- Inventory reconciliation
- Shrinkage patterns
- Adjustment behavior
- SKU-location variance
- Investigation prioritization

**Project:** [projects/inventory-silent-loss](projects/inventory-silent-loss/)

# 📂 Repository Philosophy

These projects are designed as **original portfolio concepts with uncommon analytical framing**.

I do not claim that nobody anywhere has ever explored a similar problem. Instead, originality is demonstrated through:
- the problem framing;
- custom analytical models;
- relational data structures;
- portfolio-defined KPIs;
- reproducible SQL/Python workflows;
- decision-oriented dashboards;
- documented assumptions and limitations.

Where projects use synthetic data, that is explicitly documented rather than presented as official operational data.
