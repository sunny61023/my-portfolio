# Analytical Framework

## 1. Journey Reliability

A journey is evaluated as a connected sequence of segments rather than a single delay record.

## 2. Transfer Friction

Transfer friction combines the available transfer window with the observed transfer time and disruption exposure.

## 3. Missed-Connection Risk

A connection is considered operationally vulnerable when disruption or delay consumes a material portion of the planned transfer window.

## 4. Recurring Disruptions

Repeated disruption types are grouped by station, segment, time period and severity to identify patterns that isolated delay reporting can hide.

## 5. Friction Score

The final portfolio metric can combine normalized delay, transfer pressure, waiting time and disruption recurrence. The exact weighting should be documented and tested rather than presented as an official railway metric.
